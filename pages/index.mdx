## 👋 About Me

I'm a senior software engineer with **6+ years of experience** building production backend and applied AI systems across **airline, e-commerce, and healthcare platforms**. I work remotely from **Lahore, Pakistan** and collaborate with teams in the United States, Belgium, Europe, and Latin America.

At **OnService.AI**, I build Python services and LLM-powered passenger experiences for airline clients including **World2Fly** and **LASER Airlines**. My work connects conversational agents with **KIU PSS** and airline systems for booking information, payments, check-in, flight changes, ancillary services, and passenger support.

Previously at **Trafilea**, I built a production NL-to-SQL agent used by leadership and marketing teams to query campaign data across **30+ tables**. I also architected a hierarchical LangGraph budget copilot reasoning over approximately **$5M in monthly advertising spend** across brands, countries, and channels.

Before that, I spent four years at **Awell Health**, owning core APIs and event-driven systems that supported **100K+ clinical workflows per month** as the company grew beyond **$1M ARR**.

I care about reliable systems, thoughtful architecture, developer velocity, and translating complex technical work into useful products.
